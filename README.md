🧭 QUESTRA
AI-Powered Trading Quest

Turn trading into a quest. Build discipline. Master risk. Level up.

QUESTRA is a gamified AI-powered trading education and paper-trading platform designed to help traders build real-world trading discipline through interactive challenges, AI tutoring, risk-management systems, progress tracking, and simulated trading.

Instead of simply teaching trading concepts, QUESTRA turns the learning process into a 3-stage progression system where every decision contributes to your trader profile, XP, discipline score, streaks, badges, and skill progression.

✨ What is QUESTRA?

QUESTRA combines:

🤖 AI-powered trading education
🎮 Gamified learning & progression
📈 Paper trading / simulated trading
🛡️ Risk-management enforcement
🧠 Trading psychology & discipline training
👁️ AI-assisted chart analysis
🎙️ Voice-enabled AI tutor
📓 Trade journal & post-mortems
🏆 XP, levels, quests, badges & streaks
📊 Trader analytics & account dashboard

The goal isn't to encourage users to trade more.

The goal is to train users to trade better.
